# Can a pointing model identify a passenger’s drop-off space?

**MolmoPoint-8B located all five visible vehicles correctly. It missed all six requested ground regions and returned a point on all five photos where the request should have been withheld.** Keep it out of automatic drop-off placement on the strength of this trial. A vehicle point can help locate a named landmark, although this small study cannot establish how often that would improve the full application. On the white-sedan photo, the model marked the car when asked for the vacant shoulder behind it. Its prediction for the shoulder was 10.4 source pixels from its separate prediction for the sedan and 213 pixels outside the shoulder reference. In the garage it correctly identified the bright blue car, then highlighted the central aisle when asked for the occupied bay. Across the six positive ground requests, the native predictions landed 74–772 pixels outside the specified regions. The two closest misses were at the hotel driveway and open forecourt. The other cases included a pin in the through lane and one on the named car itself. AI2 released the checkpoint in March 2026 and reports 70.7% average PointBench accuracy and 89.2 F1 on PixMo-Points. It predicts visual features directly and decodes them into image coordinates. Our earlier frontage experiment used the distinct Molmo2-8B checkpoint. For this study we ran AI2’s published MolmoPoint-8B weights at revision `188130f961c8e0888a34e11121a1423c461a01ba`, with its official constrained point decoder. All 16 requests completed on one NVIDIA A100 40 GB. Median time per request was 1.78 seconds, ranging from 1.46 to 3.20 seconds. Loading the weights took 36.10 seconds. The remote job took 65.51 seconds in total and used 18.49 GiB of peak allocated GPU memory. The model received one original photograph and a fixed instruction per trial. No masks, tracks, reference polygons or expected answers entered the prompt. The five object polygons were drawn before inference. The eleven photographs and passenger targets had been examined in earlier work, so these are development results.
