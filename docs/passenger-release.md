# Run the passenger drop-off and footage review application

The public-photo option adds a parking-surface check for named places and visible bays. Its evaluation includes automatic pins, missed locations, passenger corrections and measured start-up time. [Setup and results](passenger-places-and-gaps.md)

From the prepared laptop checkout, run the following command and open the local page. Each of the two attached recordings has 150 frames. Ten scenes are bookmarked in both sections. The passenger view also opens the optional two-car photograph when installed. Select **Open another frame in this recording** to choose a different recorded frame. The first request that needs Qwen loads the 8B model.

```powershell
.\.venv-grounding-local\Scripts\python.exe -m tools.passenger_release start --require-footage 2 --low-memory --parking-backend modal --public-places --port 8775 --vision-cache runs/model-cache/airport-segformer-b0
```

[Open the local application](http://127.0.0.1:8775/)

The prepared laptop opens the installed UAE photo bank automatically. The release command checks two distinct searchable recordings before starting. On the 4 GB laptop, run one server. The low-memory option releases the 8B worker after each passenger inference and preserves the dialogue and proposal.

For a request between two vehicles, the app checks the current road surface, both vehicle footprints and other detected road users before offering a point. The UAE photo trial records when it asked again and where alternative methods pointed. [Road-area checks and trial results](passenger-uae-road-check.md)

For automatic two-vehicle drop-off proposals, start with `--parking-backend modal`. The regular form accepts typed instructions and reviewed voice transcripts. It can identify both vehicles, mark a patch for confirmation or ask the passenger to clarify. A passenger can also mark a visible parking bay on the original photo. The driver assesses clearance, traffic and permission to stop. [Run the parking feature and see the UAE results](passenger-parking-feature.md) · [Earlier comparison on ten UAE drives](passenger-gap-diagnosis.md)

For a request about one parked car, the blue outline identifies a visible vehicle. A gold ring identifies a possible destination for passenger confirmation. A blue diamond records the passenger’s own tap. An outlined vehicle alone means the passenger still needs to identify a spot. The app can ask for confirmation of the vehicle when its position on the road and its position in the photo differ. On the prepared public gallery, 72 of the 81 photographs have stored neutral vehicle readings. The separately tested model for ground beside vehicles remains disabled after it repeatedly accepted occupied points and traffic lanes. [Project results and limits](project-release.md)

When speech recognition writes a phrase such as “behind the white card,” the app asks whether you meant the car. Choose **Yes, I meant a car**, correct the words, or choose **No, something else**. When a vehicle's position in the photo is ambiguous, choose **Yes, that vehicle** or describe a different one. Both interactions keep your original request visible. A suggested destination has its own selection and save steps.

## Try the passenger interaction

1. Select recording **054907**, frame **1**. Choose **Record voice** and say “Drop me here.” Check the recognized words and send them. The assistant should ask for a landmark.
2. Choose **Answer in words**, then reply “I'd like the patch of road just behind the white SUV on the left.” If the recognizer hears “as you v,” edit it to “SUV” before sending it. When the screen shows a proposed point, confirm it, cancel it, or mark a correction.
3. Start a new request on the same frame. Try “I mean the open road between the white SUV on our left and the large open-sided truck on our right, just behind them.” The two resolved vehicle positions constrain the proposed road point.
4. In recording **054604**, frame **150**, try “Drop me beside the yellow bus on the left.” A confirmation applies to the exact proposal on the displayed frame. Changing the proposal invalidates its previous confirmation.

When you can see a parking bay, type your description or review your transcript, then choose **Mark my spot on the photo**. Tap the spot or drag around it and choose **Check my spot**. The app checks the exact centre, the marked area and the nearby surface against parking pixels on the original photo. A current object box over the mark, a traffic lane, mixed ground or unavailable parking evidence triggers a request to choose again. A supported mark stays at the precise centre you selected. Saving the pin records which location the passenger wants. A still photo cannot establish whether the driver may stop there.

The passenger speaks English or types. The prepared laptop uses Whisper Small in English on its CPU and shows the transcript for editing. Qwen3-VL-8B sees the original frame, the passenger's words and current detector boxes. For an explicitly named pair it resolves each description against the same image. A learned referent ranker helps identify requests that still match multiple vehicles. A separately fitted model estimates a destination relative to the resolved landmarks. Predicted road pixels and the positions of visible road users constrain the point. The passenger makes the final choice. [Measured browser grounding run](../experiments/dropoff/passenger-release-browser-results-20261122.json)

An automatic pin estimates a single location. Its outline shows the model's approximate image region and can overlap scene objects. For a manual tap, the blue pin stays at the submitted point. If the check fails, a cross shows where the passenger tapped and the screen asks for a different location. Saving an offered pin records passenger intent. The driver remains responsible for vehicle control, stopping permission and physical clearance.

A passenger can know which entrance or stretch of curb they mean after the navigation app has already reached the address. Martinez-Sanchez and Greer's November 2026 preprint describes this last part of a journey and tests whether driving models respond to changed passenger wording in the same scene. Their models sometimes followed the visual scene despite the changed instruction. In this project, keeping the photo fixed while changing the request lets the team test whether its own system distinguishes the intended landmark. ## Start on another Windows machine

Sign in to GitHub CLI with an account that has access to `A1Prep/TrackRAG-Gulf` to download the private release files. Run `gh repo view A1Prep/TrackRAG-Gulf` to check access. Use Python 3.11 and the CUDA environment instructions for the pinned Qwen release. From the repository root in that activated environment, install the speech, recording and optional hosted-answer dependencies. The base model download is around 7.24 GB. [Local 8B installation](dropoff-local-training.md)

